
# Wormhole-Chess

A collection of chess variants, currently hosted at wormhole-chess.com

## Intro

Current modes: wormhole-chess, deckbuilder-chess

## Details

### wormhole-chess

This mode has 2 connected boards for up to 4 players.  
Players play in order 1 to 4, and normal chess rules apply.  
When a player is in check, they are unable to move unless their move would free them from check (i.e. *checkmate*).
> *Checkmate may be removed in subsequent turns, allowing the player to make moves again.*  
To win, a player must simultaneously checkmate all 3 opponents.

Pawns are promoted when they reach any final rank or file.
> This specification is necessary in the case of, for example, *c-file* or *d-file* pawns, which may change direction by traversing the wormhole.  

### deckbuilder-chess

This mode allows players to play cards from their hand in combination with their standard chess moves.  
Players prepare 5 or 8 card hands to bring into a match.  
Players earn 1 energy per turn and spend energy to play cards.
