      1. `P1 (Sidestep)`: Blocked frontally -> steps 1 square diagonally forward onto empty square; rejects if not blocked frontally.
      2. `P3 (March Together)`: Two horizontally adjacent friendly Pawns with empty front squares -> both advance 1 square forward.
      3. `P4 (Attack Together)`: Pawn captures enemy diagonally while a friendly Pawn is anywhere in its 8 surrounding squares -> adjacent Pawn moves into vacated square.
      4. `P5 (Get Behind Me)`: Friendly non-Pawn piece in front/forward-diagonal of Pawn is under attack -> swaps places with Pawn.
      5. `P6 (Double Time)`: Pawn on Rank 3, 4, or 5 with 2 empty squares ahead -> advances 2 squares straight forward; rejects on Rank 2 or Rank 6.
      6. `P7 (Protect the King)`: Pawn with friendly King in its 8 surrounding squares -> slides diagonally any unobstructed distance to move or capture.
      7. `P8 (Call Reserves)`: Pawn on Ranks 5–7 with empty rear-diagonal square -> spawns friendly Pawn on rear-diagonal square.
      8. `B1 (Let Me Through)`: Friendly Pawn on Bishop's forward diagonal -> Bishop phases through Pawn to empty or enemy capture square beyond it (Pawn stays put).
      9. `B2 (Pierce)`: Enemy Pawn on Bishop's diagonal -> Bishop destroys enemy Pawn and lands on empty square OR captures enemy piece beyond it.
      10. `B4 (Change Color)`: Enemy piece orthogonally adjacent to Bishop -> Bishop steps 1 square orthogonally (to empty or capturing that enemy), switching color.
      11. `B5 (Take My Place)`: Bishop under attack -> swaps with friendly non-King piece along unobstructed diagonal.
      12. `B6 (Diagonal Launch)`: Friendly Pawn on any of the 3 squares in the rank ahead of Bishop -> launches Pawn along Pawn's forward diagonals (move or capture).
      13. `B7 (Bishop's Blessing)`: Friendly non-Bishop piece in Bishop's 8 surrounding squares -> slides unobstructed along diagonals of its current square (move or capture).
      14. `N2 (Springboard)`: Friendly piece on Knight's L-jump square `S1` -> Knight bounces off `S1` to make a second L-jump to `S2`.
      15. `N3 (Trample)`: Supports both `2x1` and `1x2` L-paths, allows empty or capture landing, and populates `arrows` metadata.
      16. `N4 (Square Jump)`: Knight defended by any friendly piece -> jumps `(±2, ±2)` diagonally to move or capture.
      17. `N5 (Get to the King)`: Knight jumps to unthreatened square `S1` (empty or capture) that forks enemy King + another enemy piece, then jumps from `S1` to `S2`; rejects if `S1` is threatened by an enemy piece.
      18. `N6 (Flanking Support)`: Works from `a, b, g, or h` files.
      19. `N7 (Outpost Support)`: Works on Ranks 5–8 without requiring an existing defending Pawn.
      20. `R2 (I've Got Your Back)`: Pushes ally on same rank 1 square forward to empty OR enemy capture, Rook slides into vacated square.
      21. `R4 (Move in Unison)`: Connected Rooks -> one Rook moves along rank/file (move or capture) and second Rook moves into first Rook's vacated square.
      22. `R5 (Catapult)`: Friendly non-King piece directly in front of Rook (`N/S/E/W`) launched along open ray to capture enemy piece ahead.
      23. `R6 (Garrison)`: Rook orthogonally adjacent (`N/E/S/W`) to King spawns Pawn 1 rank in front of Rook.
      24. `Q1 (Royal Summons)`: Pulls any friendly non-King piece in 8-directional sight (`distance >= 2`) onto empty square adjacent to Queen.
      25. `Q2 (The Queen's Gambit)`: Includes 2 preview arrows (`Queen -> Ally` and `Ally -> Attacker`).
      26. `Q3 (Royal Shield)`: Friendly Pawn 1 square away along ray -> Pawn lands on target `T` (move or capture) and Queen lands on `T - dir`.
      27. `Q4 (One Fell Swoop)`: Works with any number of empty squares between supporting `R/B/Q` and Queen.
      28. `K1 (Conscription)`: Works from any square on Rank 1, spawning Pawns on empty `N/E/W` orthogonal squares.
      29. `K3 (Royal Execution)`: Enemy in King's 8-directional sightline + ally in King's 8 surrounding squares -> ally launches onto enemy square to capture.
      30. `K4 (Turncoat)`: Enemy Pawn in King's 8 surrounding squares converts in-place into a friendly Pawn.

      - `p3_follow_me` (`March Together`): `minAnchorsRequired: 2`. Checks `(file ± 1, rank)` for a friendly Pawn where both `(file, rank + dir)` and `(file ± 1, rank + dir)` are empty -> advances both Pawns 1 square forward (`to = (file, rank + dir)`, `extraSquare = (file ± 1, rank)`).
      - `p4_attack_together`: Checks all 8 surrounding squares (`chebyshev === 1`) of `anchorSquare` for another friendly Pawn (`extraSquare`), and forward diagonals `(file ± 1, rank + dir)` for a non-King enemy piece (`to`). Captures enemy at `to` and moves friendly Pawn from `extraSquare` to `anchorSquare`.
      - `p5_get_behind_me`: Checks the 3 forward squares `(file - 1..file + 1, rank + dir)` for a friendly non-Pawn, non-King piece that is currently attacked by an enemy piece (`isSquareAttackedBy`). Swaps the Pawn at `anchorSquare` with that attacked friendly piece at `to`.
      - `p6_tag_out` (`Double Time`): Checks `relRank` is `2, 3, or 4` (Ranks 3, 4, or 5) and both `(file, rank + dir)` and `(file, rank + 2 * dir)` are empty and not blocked by barriers -> moves Pawn to `(file, rank + 2 * dir)`.
      - `p7_protect_the_king`: Checks if friendly King is in the 8 surrounding squares (`chebyshev === 1`) of `anchorSquare`. Scans all 4 diagonals from `anchorSquare` for unobstructed empty squares or first enemy non-King capture -> moves Pawn to `to`.
      - `p8_call_reserves`: Checks `relRank` in `4..6` (Ranks 5–7) and `engine.canSpawnPawn(turn)`. Targets empty rear-diagonal squares `(file ± 1, rank - dir)` -> spawns a friendly Pawn on `to`.
    - **Bishops**:
      - `b1_let_me_through`: Checks forward diagonals (`df = ±1, dr = dir`). If a friendly Pawn sits along that forward diagonal with no pieces between the Bishop and that Pawn, scans beyond that Pawn along the same diagonal for unobstructed empty squares or first enemy non-King capture -> moves Bishop to `to` (friendly Pawn remains untouched).
      - `b2_pierce`: Scans all 4 diagonals. Once exactly 1 enemy Pawn is encountered at `pawnSq`, scans beyond `pawnSq` along the same diagonal: adds every unobstructed empty square AND the first enemy non-King piece encountered (`to`, with `extraSquare: pawnSq`). On execute, removes the enemy Pawn at `extraSquare`, removes any enemy at `to`, and places Bishop at `to`.
      - `b4_change_color`: Checks if at least 1 enemy piece is orthogonally adjacent (`N/S/E/W`, distance 1) to `anchorSquare`. If so, targets all orthogonally adjacent squares (`N/S/E/W`) that are either empty or occupied by a non-King enemy piece.
      - `b5_take_my_place`: Checks if the Bishop at `anchorSquare` is currently under standard enemy attack (`isSquareAttackedBy`). Scans all 4 diagonals for unobstructed friendly non-King pieces -> swaps Bishop with chosen friendly piece at `to`.
      - `b6_come_this_way` (`Diagonal Launch`): Checks the 3 squares in the rank ahead of Bishop (`(file - 1, rank + dir)`, `(file, rank + dir)`, `(file + 1, rank + dir)`) for a friendly Pawn at `pawnSq`. For each such Pawn, scans that Pawn's two forward diagonals (`df = ±1, dr = dir`) from `pawnSq` for unobstructed empty squares or first enemy non-King capture (`to`, with `extraSquare: pawnSq`, `disambiguationPrompt: 'Choose Pawn to launch'`). On execute, moves the Pawn from `extraSquare` to `to` (capturing any enemy at `to`) while Bishop stays at `anchorSquare`.
      - `b7_bishops_blessing`: Finds all friendly non-Bishop, non-King (or including King if safe) pieces in the 8 surrounding squares (`chebyshev === 1`) of `anchorSquare`. For each adjacent ally at `allySq`, scans all 4 diagonals from `allySq` (ignoring obstacles only if open; stops at first piece, capturing if enemy non-King) -> `to`, `extraSquare: allySq`, `disambiguationPrompt: 'Choose blessed ally'`. On execute, moves ally from `extraSquare` to `to` while Bishop stays at `anchorSquare`.
