  <action>
    1. In `src/components/DeckChessBoard.tsx`:

       - When a new move arrives (and `isViewingHistory === false`):
         - Compute coordinate delta $(\Delta x, \Delta y)$ between `lastMoveFrom` and `lastMoveTo` in percentage of square width/height (taking board orientation `w` vs `b` into account).
