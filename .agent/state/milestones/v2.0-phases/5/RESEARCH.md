# Phase 5 Research: Deck Builder Card Graph & Power Taxonomy

## Executive Summary

Phase 5 transitions Deck Builder Chess from a prototype with 12 loosely defined cards into a mathematically structured, tournament-grade tactical engine. Built upon a 6×6 Condition-to-Effect grammar matrix, two symmetrical 3-cycles, and a unified action model ("The Card IS the Move"), Phase 5 implements the first canonical 90-card vertical slice (covering Pawn, Knight, and Bishop), enforces all chess invariants, and integrates the glowing gold empowered-move board interaction.

---

## 1. Architectural Taxonomy: 6×6 Grammar Matrix

Every tactical card in the game is deterministically generated by:
$$\text{Piece Anchor} \times \text{Condition Genre} \longrightarrow \text{Effect Genre}$$

### 1.1 The 6 Condition Genres (5 Concrete Triggers Each)
1. **Position**: Corner (`a1, h1, a8, h8`), Center (`d4, e4, d5, e5`), Back Rank (`rank 1 / 8`), Rim/Edge (`a/h files`, `ranks 1/8`), Enemy Territory (`ranks 5-8` for White, `1-4` for Black).
2. **Neighbors**: Orthogonal Ally, Diagonal Ally, Specific Ally, Partner, Formation.
3. **Lines**: Open File, Open Rank, Open Diagonal, Direct Stare (same piece class), Sightline to King.
4. **Threat**: Under Fire, Delivering Check, Forking, Hunting High Value, Contested (Crossfire).
5. **Blocked**: Absolute Pin, Low Mobility ($\le 2$ legal moves), Covered Squares, Defended Target, Friendly in Way.
6. **Momentum**: Starting Square, Just Moved Last Turn, Long Dormant ($3+$ turns), Captured Last Turn, Consecutive Moves.

### 1.2 The 6 Effect Genres (Tactile Chess Board Themes)
1. **New Moves**: Gaining foreign movement vectors (Bishop stepping orthogonally to invert color, Pawn stepping sideways, Rook stepping diagonally, Knight sliding).
2. **Jump & Leap**: Bypassing obstacles, fairy chess leaps (Camel `(3,1)`, Zebra `(3,2)`, Dabbaba `(2,0)`), rim-to-rim cylindrical wrap-around, hit-and-run recoil.
3. **Move Others**: Coordinate swaps, passenger transport, tandem shifts, pushing/pulling allies.
4. **Special Captures**: Piercing rays through lines, straight pawn captures, ranged strikes without moving, rear-kick and trample captures.
5. **Walls & Shields**: Deploying 1-2 turn holographic barriers on empty squares, kinetic shields protecting pieces against captures.
6. **Spawning**: Summoning fresh pawns, graveyard resurrection onto valid ranks (ranks 2–7).

## 2. Condition Affinities: Symmetrical 3-Cycles

Each piece specializes in **1 Condition** ("Good At") and excludes **1 Condition** ("Bad At"):

### Battlefield Minor Units (Pawn $\leftrightarrow$ Knight $\leftrightarrow$ Bishop)
$$\text{Pawn (Blocked)} \xrightarrow{\text{Excl. Momentum}} \text{Knight (Momentum)} \xrightarrow{\text{Excl. Lines}} \text{Bishop (Lines)} \xrightarrow{\text{Excl. Blocked}} \text{Pawn}$$
* **Pawn**: Good at `Blocked` | Excludes `Momentum`
* **Knight**: Good at `Momentum` | Excludes `Lines`
* **Bishop**: Good at `Lines` | Excludes `Blocked`

### Royal Court Major Units (Rook $\leftrightarrow$ King $\leftrightarrow$ Queen)
$$\text{Rook (Neighbors)} \xrightarrow{\text{Excl. Position}} \text{King (Position)} \xrightarrow{\text{Excl. Threat}} \text{Queen (Threat)} \xrightarrow{\text{Excl. Neighbors}} \text{Rook}$$
* **Rook**: Good at `Neighbors` | Excludes `Position`
* **King**: Good at `Position` | Excludes `Threat`
* **Queen**: Good at `Threat` | Excludes `Neighbors`

## 3. The 30-Cards-Per-Piece Formula (180 Total Library)

* Each piece has **5 active condition genres** ($6 - 1 \text{ excluded}$).
* The "Good At" condition branches into **2 distinct effect genres** ($5 \text{ scenarios} \times 2 = 10 \text{ cards}$).
* The other 4 condition genres map to **1 effect genre each** ($4 \times 5 = 20 \text{ cards}$).
* **Total per piece**: $10 + 20 = 30 \text{ cards}$.
* **All 6 Effect Genres** are active for every piece.
* **Energy Distribution**: Balanced bell curve across all pieces:
  * `1⚡`: 12 cards (40%) — fast tactical utility & minor repositions.
  * `2⚡`: 12 cards (40%) — core workhorses & battlefield control.
  * `3⚡`: 6 cards (20%) — high-impact game-altering apex abilities.

## 4. Unified Action Model: "The Card IS the Move"

### Problem with Legacy Model
Previously, turns were split into:
$$\text{Play Card (Pre-move window)} \longrightarrow \text{Make Chess Move}$$
This caused severe balance issues (playing an ability on the queenside, then making an unrelated checkmate on the kingside), UI ambiguity, and awkward multi-stage targeting dialogs.

### The Unified Solution ([DECISION-019])
Every turn in Deck Builder Chess is unified into a single atomic action:
$$\text{On your turn: Make a Standard Move (0⚡) } \mathbf{OR} \text{ Make an Empowered Card Move (1–3⚡)}$$

1. **Board Interaction**:
   - Clicking a piece on `DeckChessBoard` calculates both:
     - Standard legal chess moves $\rightarrow$ rendered with subtle gray/green dots (`0⚡`).
     - Empowered card moves unlocked by cards in hand $\rightarrow$ rendered with glowing gold rings (`1–3⚡`).
2. **Execution**:
   - Clicking an empowered gold tile executes the augmented move (or targeted in-place action), deducts the card's energy cost, removes the single-use card from the player's active hand dock, and advances the turn atomically.

## 5. Critical Invariants & Edge-Case Safeguards

1. **Pawn Rank Boundaries (FEN Invariant)**:
   - `isValidPawnSquare(square)` strictly enforces ranks 2 through 7 for all pawns.
   - Spawns behind or beside pawns/bishops/knights explicitly guard against rank 1 placement.
2. **Rank 8 Promotion**:
   - When a pawn reaches rank 8 via an empowered card move (e.g., *Bayonet Charge*, *Pike Thrust*, *Vaulting Advance*), standard chess promotion to Queen applies.
3. **No-Direct-Mate Invariant**:
   - Because the card *is* the move, all standard chess rules (resolving checks, preventing illegal king exposure, pin-line safety) apply directly during move generation.
4. **Kinetic Shield Migration**:
   - When a shielded piece moves, its kinetic shield modifier follows it to the destination square.
5. **Barrier Collisions**:
   - Holographic barriers block all straight/diagonal sliding lines, pawn double-steps, and king castling transit.
